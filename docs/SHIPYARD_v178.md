# B079 / v1.78 Shipyard quick start

1. Open **Home → build a new task force**, or the **Shipyard** tab.
2. Choose a flagship. Add S/M escorts and their quantities before choosing optional supply support.
3. Choose an auxiliary such as a Nomad, or continue without one.
4. Select the assembly sector. **Save as template** stores the composition without ordering.
5. Choose your route:
   - **Review equipment and complete price** purchases from NPC yards.
   - **Review owned-yard construction** uses your stations and their resources; the review identifies your yards and shows no credit charge.
   - **Review buy / build together** combines routes, preferring NPC suppliers where available.
6. Review each ship entry. **Edit equipment / crew / saved loadout** offers compatible saved loadouts and supported equipment choices. Check service crew and marine requests against ship capacity and available Academy personnel. Saved loadouts do not import extra crew or paint.
7. Confirm once. Checking prepared order/delivery reads status; it does not buy again.
8. Follow delivery, assembly and rally status. Submitted orders are not completed ships. Use **Fleets** for the completed fleet's operational Home and assignment.

## Known workflow limits

- Adding escorts from the summary is disabled after selecting an auxiliary. Add escorts first.
- Combined ordering is not an own-yard-first policy; explicitly choose the own-yard route for that.
- A changed supplier quote requires a fresh review before confirmation.
- An existing purchase reservation may block another order. Check the retained order; do not repeatedly confirm or assume a refusal proves the previous order never happened.
- The optional capture policy attempts suitable boarding, not guaranteed captures.

Delivery, crew-transfer outcome, assembly/rally and reload were not confirmed by that observation. Release preparation proceeded at the author's direction without waiting for delivery.
